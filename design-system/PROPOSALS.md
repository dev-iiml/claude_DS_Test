### P-01 — attribute states (detail)

| State           | Mark                        | Wording pattern                  | Example                                               |
| --------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Self-declared   | `circle-dot`, ink-subtle    | "Self-declared"                  | Legal name · Self-declared                            |
| Verified        | `circle-check`, success     | "Verified · {source}, {date}"    | Creator activity · Verified · Instagram, 3 Oct        |
| Derived         | `sparkles`, info            | "Derived from {verified inputs}" | Posting consistency · Derived from connected accounts |
| Missing         | `circle-dashed`, ink-subtle | "Not provided · {action}"        | Mobile number · Not provided · Add                    |
| Needs attention | `circle-alert`, warning     | "{what changed} · {fix}"         | Handle differs from what you declared · Review        |

Rules: verification applies to individual attributes; never call the whole Passport or the creator "verified" when only some attributes are — say "3 of 6 details verified". Keep professional status and signals separate from financial eligibility: _"Status is about your creator career — it never sets financial limits."_

### P-03 — progress and availability (detail)

1. Every incomplete task leads to an **available action**. If the action is not available yet, it is not counted against the creator and not listed as outstanding.
2. Unavailable capabilities are explained without implying the creator did them: "Coming in the next build", "Not available in your region yet".
3. **Onboarding completion** is finite (6 steps → Complete, never re-opens). **Passport strength** is ongoing (profile details, connections, verifications) and is not a score.
4. **Today's 80% cap:** `computeProfileCompletion` gives "Connect a creator account" 20%, but connecting is not part of onboarding, so a creator finishes onboarding at 80% and Home shows "Complete your Passport — 80%". Resolve by (a) showing onboarding as complete at the consent step and (b) moving the connection weight into Passport strength, where "Connect an account" is the available next action.
