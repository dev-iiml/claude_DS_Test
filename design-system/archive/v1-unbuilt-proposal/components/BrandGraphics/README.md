# BrandGraphics

Two brand graphics, both quiet: **the Door** and **the Rani dot**. Plus the direction for creator photography.

## The Door
An arch — a rectangle with a semicircular head — drawn as a single hairline. It carries the brand line "Your influence opens doors" and nods to Indian architecture without ornament. Use it on the invitation screen, the status reveal, card art and empty states. One door per view; never filled, never patterned, never as a frame around people.

## The Rani dot
The full stop after the wordmark. As a graphic it marks *the one thing that is yours* — a new invitation, an unread status moment. Max one per screen.

## Photography
- Real Indian creators at work: studios, kitchens, streets, sets — editorial, natural light, unposed, diverse in region, body, gender and age.
- Crop tight on hands, tools, process; faces when the person is the story.
- Warm, slightly desaturated grade; never neon, never stock-smile.
- Never show follower counts, ring lights as hero, or money as a prop (cash fans, cards in hand).
- Placeholders in this system are hatched blocks labelled "Creator photo".
