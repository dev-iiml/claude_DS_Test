# Wordmark

The wordmark is a **placeholder**: "CreatorCo" set in the display face with a Rani dot. Replace it in one place when the real name and mark exist.

## Use
- Top-left of the top bar and sidebar; centred on the invitation screen; top-left on card art.
- Sizes: 24px (default), 19px (`wm-sm`, card art and compact bars), up to 56px on the invitation hero.
- The dot is the only coloured part: `accent` on product surfaces, the tier mark colour on card art.
- Show the dashed "placeholder" tag in design reviews only — never in production builds.

## Swapping it
Everything references the `Wordmark` component (`wm` class in previews). Replace its contents with the final SVG lockup; keep a clear space of one dot-width × 3 on all sides and a minimum height of 16px.

## Don't
- Don't recolour the word, add a crest, monogram, laurel or any heritage ornament.
- Don't borrow any card issuer's naming, typography or trade dress.
