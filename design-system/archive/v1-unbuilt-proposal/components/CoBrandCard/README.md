# CoBrandCard

Art system for the future co-branded payment card (physical metal + virtual). It is a **financial product of the partner**, so it follows finance rules, not status rules.

## Rules
- Mandatory zones: "Issued by [Partner Bank]" (top-right, ≥ 7pt on metal), network mark zone (bottom-right, sized to network rules), EMV chip zone.
- Finishes are a cardholder choice — **Graphite** (`card-graphite`) or **Chalk** (`card-chalk`) — and are **never tied to Creator Status**. No Onyx/Icon materials on a payment card.
- The CreatorCo wordmark is smaller than on the membership card; the partner's legal name is always legible.
- No limit, no tier name, no "credit" wording on the art.
