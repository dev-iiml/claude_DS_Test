# Accent usage

Two accents, two jobs. Both are rare by design — that rarity is what makes them feel expensive.

## Rani (`accent`) — the funky one

**Use for:** the wordmark dot · the invitation screen (Door line + "Accept invitation") · the status reveal · the Icon tier's single line · one italic flourish or full stop per screen ("Aarohi.") · text selection.

**Rules**
- One Rani element per view. If a second one appears, demote it to `text` or `brass`.
- Rani buttons (`btn-signature`) only for membership moments: accept invitation, reveal status. Never for "Continue", "Submit" or anything financial.
- Never on: eligibility results, eligibility consent, charts, errors, the risk console, payment-card art.
- Text on a Rani fill uses `on-accent`. Rani as text needs `canvas`, `surface` or `accent-soft` behind it.

## Brass (`brass`) — the classy one

**Use for:** the Member tier mark · "Repeat" partner tags · member-benefit icons · editorial eyebrows on membership pages · chart series 1 and the sequential ramp.

**Rules**
- Brass is detail, not fill: badges, hairlines, icons, small type. The only large brass area is the cover.
- Never gold-on-black filigree, metallic gradients or foil effects in UI.

## Quick test

Squint at the screen. You should see neutrals first, one spark of Rani or brass second, and nothing that looks like a sale.
