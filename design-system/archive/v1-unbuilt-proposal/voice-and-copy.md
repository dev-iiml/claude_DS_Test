# Voice and copy

**Confident, warm, a little witty. Never pushy about money.** Speak like a well-connected friend who happens to understand finance: short sentences, plain words, a wink where it's earned.

## Tone by moment

| Moment | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Invitation, status | Quietly proud, a touch of swagger | "You've been *invited.*" · "Recognised for four years of verified, consistent brand work." |
| Access | Energetic, specific | "Doors open *this week.*" · "40 places · Mumbai · 12 Oct" |
| Onboarding | Reassuring, brief | "First, your number. We'll text a code. No spam, no calls — promise." |
| Consent | Clear, respectful | "Choose what we can use — and why." · "Say yes to one without the others." |
| Financial eligibility | Calm, transparent, neutral | "Based on your Financial Capacity: 14 months of consistent brand income." |
| Errors | Helpful, no blame | "That code didn't match. Try again or resend." · "This is on our side. Your data is safe." |
| Empty states | Light wit | "The queue is clear. Nice." |

## Mechanics

- Sentence case everywhere; `eyebrow` style does the uppercase.
- "You" for the creator, "we" for CreatorCo. Name the partner when it acts.
- Verbs first on buttons, ≤ 3 words: "Request access", "View receipt", "Check financial eligibility".
- Indian English spelling (recognised, programme, personalised); Indian number and date formats.
- No emoji in product UI. Em dash and middle dot (·) as separators.
- Wit lives in empty states, confirmations and onboarding — never in consent, eligibility, errors about money, or the risk console.

## Approved terms

Check financial eligibility · CreatorCo recommendation · Proposed eligibility · Subject to partner-lender approval · Creator Status · Creator Professional Profile · Creator Passport · Financial Capacity · Member · Onyx · Icon · Access · Issued by [Partner Bank].

## Never write

- "CreatorCo approved your loan" / "approved" in any CreatorCo voice
- "Guaranteed credit", "Instant loan", "Pre-approved"
- "Followers determine your credit" or any link between audience size and money
- "AI says you deserve ₹X"
- "Increase your status to get more debt", "Level up to unlock a bigger limit"
- The word "bank" in the CreatorCo brand itself (the partner's name may contain it)
- Countdown pressure: "Only 2 hours left!", "Hurry"

## Hindi

Hindi is a full translation in natural, contemporary Hindi (Hinglish loanwords where creators actually use them: प्रोफ़ाइल, प्लैटफ़ॉर्म), not a literal word-for-word rendering. Approved anchors:

| English | हिन्दी |
|---|---|
| Check financial eligibility | वित्तीय पात्रता जाँचें |
| Subject to partner-lender approval | भागीदार ऋणदाता की स्वीकृति के अधीन |
| Your data, your call | आपका डेटा, आपका फ़ैसला |
| I agree | मैं सहमत हूँ |
| Not now | अभी नहीं |
| Withdraw | वापस लें |
| View receipt | रसीद देखें |
| Continue | आगे बढ़ें |

Legal and consent text in Hindi must be reviewed by a qualified translator before release.
