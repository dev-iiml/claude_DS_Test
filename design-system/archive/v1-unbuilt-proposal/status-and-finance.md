# Status and finance

Creator Status measures **professional standing**. Financial eligibility is a **recommendation** prepared for a regulated partner. They are designed as two different worlds so that no creator ever reads a status upgrade as more credit, or credit as status.

## Two visual languages

| | Creator Status | Financial eligibility |
|---|---|---|
| Colour | Tier materials (`tier-*`), Rani, brass | Neutral ink, `surface`, `info` disclosure, `chart-2` slate data |
| Type | Display serif, mono caps on cards | Interface sans, tabular figures |
| Shape | Card art, pill badges with a tier dot | Plain bordered cards, horizontal bars |
| Motion | The reveal (rise + draw, 1.2s) | None beyond standard 180ms transitions |
| Words | Member, Onyx, Icon, recognised, invitation, access | Check financial eligibility, CreatorCo recommendation, Proposed eligibility, Financial Capacity, Subject to partner-lender approval |
| Lives in | Passport header, dashboard identity row, membership card, access requirements, status reveal | Eligibility tab, eligibility result, consent for eligibility, partner hand-off, risk console |

## Do

- Put the tier badge beside the creator's **name** — identity.
- Describe tier progress in professional terms: verified brand work, complete Passport, consistent activity.
- Show eligibility as a **range**, labelled "Proposed eligibility" and "CreatorCo recommendation", with the disclosure always visible.
- Name the partner in the hand-off: "Continue to [Partner Bank]".
- State what eligibility is based on and how to strengthen it; say plainly that follower count is not a factor.
- Keep the co-branded payment card's finish a cardholder choice (`card-graphite`, `card-chalk`).

## Don't

- Don't place a tier badge, tier material or Rani on any eligibility surface — or a ₹ amount, "limit" or eligibility copy on any status surface.
- Don't design a status upgrade as a number going up, a progress-to-credit bar, or "unlock a higher limit".
- Don't celebrate financial outcomes: no confetti, badges, sounds or "Congratulations" on eligibility.
- Don't use "approved", "guaranteed", "instant", "pre-approved" or "you deserve".
- Don't make eligibility the centre of the dashboard or the bottom-nav centre button; it sits third, after identity and Passport.
- Don't tie the payment card's finish, metal or name to Creator Status.

## Review check

Cover the page's labels. If you can still tell which tier the creator is from a finance screen, or how much they could borrow from a status screen, it fails.
